Become a partner

Build elite basketball in the north with us.

Tromsø Storm gives businesses a visible role around the game-day experience, across the club's digital channels and in an engaged Northern Norwegian basketball community. We shape each partnership around what the business wants to achieve.

Request a partnership conversation
01

Energetic visibility

Brand exposure across relevant club channels, around home games and in editorial content agreed in advance.

02

Relationships and hospitality

Opportunities to use game day to meet customers, employees and partners in a strong local setting.

03

Engaging activation

Competitions, game-host activities and content are developed with the partner and tailored to the target audience.

04

02Partnership levels

A place that matches your ambition.

Final content, scope and pricing are agreed with the club. The levels provide a clear starting point for the conversation.

Maximum presence

Main partner

For businesses seeking a central role in Storm's ambitions and a tailored programme across the club's most important channels.

  • Priority visibility
  • Tailored activation
  • Game-day and hospitality opportunities
Visible throughout the season

Partner

For businesses seeking consistent visibility and specific meeting places throughout the season.

  • Agreed digital and physical channels
  • Game-activation opportunities
  • Partner communication and hospitality
An accessible entry level

Supporting partner

For local businesses that want to support the team and be visible within the community around Tromsø Storm.

  • Visibility in the partner directory
  • Connection to the club's network
  • Opportunity for further development
How we begin

From goals to partnership.

  1. A short conversationWe identify the target audience, goals and desired level of involvement.
  2. A specific proposalThe club puts together the level, channels and activities.
  3. A clear planThe parties agree on deliverables, responsibilities and follow-up.
