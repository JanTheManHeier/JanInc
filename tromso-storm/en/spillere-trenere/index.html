8
Guard · 198 cm · born 2000

Garrett Denbow

About the player

Garrett is the final import joining the team this season. He attended Anderson University in South Carolina and then played for two G League clubs in the United States. His latest club was Rip City Remix, affiliated with the Portland Trail Blazers. He is a strong all-round player whose greatest strength is shooting, with a three-point percentage above 40% in both college and the G League. It will be exciting to see what he can do in the BLNO.

About the player

Brage is a talented player with a good shot. He is serious and dedicated, although injuries have affected him over the past year. We believe he can become very good with time. His older brother Trym is also on the team.

9 years in the top division

About the player

Aron is one of the team's true veterans. Perhaps the team's best shooter, he is also a tremendous teammate. The Harstad native is once again one of this year's key players.

Guard · 189 cm · born 2001

Trym Engelsen

5 years in the top division

About the player

Trym is becoming one of the experienced players on the team. He has made great strides in recent years and is one of the team's best shooters. He has played both in the league and the First Division, where he has particularly shown his scoring ability. We believe his strong development will continue this year.

Jakob completed his first year with the first team last season, but an ankle injury ruined the final part of his season. The son of former Storm player Jan Heier Johansen, Jakob is a guard with a good shot. He developed well over the past year, and we hope that continues this season.

David has now spent several years with the first team. He is a tall and very talented young player who has unfortunately dealt with minor injuries in recent years. He showed good development in the First Division last year, and we hope that continues in the BLNO.

About the player

Veteran Marcus is making a comeback after stepping away halfway through last season. He loves basketball and wanted to help the club return to the league. An experienced, high-quality player who, at his best, is also among the finest long-range shooters in Norwegian basketball.

Guard · 186 cm · born 2004

Mathias Hansen Nymo

3 years in the top division

About the player

Mathias is a talented young player who has spent several years with Storm, but injuries have unfortunately disrupted recent seasons. Forward · 206 cm · born 1993

Grant Sitton

About the player

Grant is one of the club's two new American players. He is from Oregon and has played in many places during college and his professional career. His latest contract was with Saitama Broncos in Japan. At 206 cm, he is a highly versatile player and an excellent shooter, including from three-point range. It will be exciting to see what he can achieve in the BLNO.
