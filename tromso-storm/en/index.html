05Roster 2026/2027

Built in the north.

Local standouts, young talent and international experience. Meet the team taking Tromsø Storm forward with dark Arena energy and a distinct identity.

4
Point guard · 182 cm · born 1998

Johannes Rydningen Lange

About the player

Our captain and one of the veterans will lead the Storm ship together with Anders Solem. He is a highly skilled point guard, vital to the team, and is also becoming a regular member of the national team.

Forward · 206 cm · born 1993

Grant Sitton

About the player

Grant is one of the club's two new American players. He is from Oregon and has played in many places during college and his professional career. His latest contract was with Saitama Broncos in Japan. At 206 cm, he is a highly versatile player and an excellent shooter, including from three-point range. It will be exciting to see what he can achieve in the BLNO.

8
Guard · 198 cm · born 2000

Garrett Denbow

About the player

Garrett is the final import joining the team this season. He attended Anderson University in South Carolina and then played for two G League clubs in the United States. His latest club was Rip City Remix, affiliated with the Portland Trail Blazers. He is a strong all-round player whose greatest strength is shooting, with a three-point percentage above 40% in both college and the G League. It will be exciting to see what he can do in the BLNO.

34
Forward · 195 cm · born 1992

Anders Solem

About the player

The super-veteran and role model of the team. Anders is a fantastic person, player and teammate, and will be important in helping the younger players progress.

06Why support Tromsø Storm?

The elite club
in the north.

Tromsø Storm is a pillar of Norwegian basketball — with roots dating back to 1976, strong national achievements and a clear role as the sport's flagship in the north.

Explore partnership opportunities
01

Elite sport in the north

A partnership with Storm connects your brand with ambition, performance and top-level basketball.

02

A club with heritage

Almost 50 years of standout players, great moments and a central place in Norwegian basketball history.

03

Visible in the local community

Home games, digital channels and an engaged basketball community create valuable meeting places throughout the season.

07Community engagement

Bigger than the game.

Tromsø Storm has worked for children and young people in the city for more than 20 years. Through its partnership with Samfunnsløftet, the club can develop more activities, meeting places and opportunities for young people in the north.

01

The joy of basketball for more people

Easier access to activity, achievement and belonging — both in Storm Ungdom and together with the basketball communities around us.

02

Local role models

Players and coaches use their elite-sport experience to inspire and support the next generation.

03

Strong values

Fair play, healthy attitudes and Clean Sports Club principles guide the work both on and off the court.
