Season tickets

Join us for every home league game and support Storm throughout the season.

Buy with Vipps
01 More Storm for your money

Your place all season

The schedule includes 13 home league games. A season ticket saves an adult NOK 330 and a student NOK 240 compared with buying a ticket for every game.

Adult 1 100 krStudent 800 kr

A valid student ID must be shown at the first home game.

02 How to buy

Pay with Vipps

  1. Open Vipps and select “Send”.
  2. Find Tromsø Storm using Vipps number 16355.
  3. Pay NOK 1,100 for an adult or NOK 800 for a student.
  4. Write Adult season ticket – full name or Student season ticket – full name in the message.

If you have questions about the purchase, contact storm@tromsostorm.no.

03 What you get

Season-ticket benefits

  • A dedicated seating area in the arena for those who want it.
  • Lower cost than buying single-game tickets.
  • A photo with the Tromsø Storm player of your choice.
  • You support the team directly throughout the season.

The season ticket is valid for Tromsø Storm's home league games in the 2026/27 season.
