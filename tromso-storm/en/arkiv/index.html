The history, people and archive of the elite club in the north.

01The club

A pillar of Norwegian basketball

Since 1976

Basketball power from the north

Tromsø Storm represents elite basketball in Northern Norway and builds on decades of standout players, great games and local belonging.

The club aims to be a clear meeting place for players, supporters, volunteers and partners.
